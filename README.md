# Summer Training -- Panki Thermal Extension Plant

## Overview

This repository documents the summer training undertaken at **Panki
Thermal Extension Plant (PTEP), 1 × 660 MW, Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh**,
under **Uttar Pradesh Rajya Vidyut Utpadan Nigam Limited (UPRVUNL)**.

The training was completed by **Divyanshi Sonkar**, a student of **Madan
Mohan Malaviya University of Technology (MMMUT), Gorakhpur**.
